# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Discuss ideas and implementation choices
without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests
or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation
when needed. For sensitive concerns, contact a maintainer privately via their GitHub
profile; do not publish personal information in a report. Security vulnerabilities
follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
